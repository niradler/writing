# HackIDC  Hackathon  2015

IKE is the #1 winning at HackIDC 2015, built in 48 hours.  
The companion for your bike. IKE is an electronic protective gear that helps the city cyclist stay safe while riding.

IKE can initiates bike signaling from your Pebble watch, warns drivers approaching from behind, and automatically reports your location and calls for help in emergencies.  
The project was chosen by the majority of HackIDC participants' to represent Israel in Global Hackathon Seoul 2015.
