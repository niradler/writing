# About

Hey there! 👋 I'm Nir Adler a developer, maker, and open-source enthusiast who loves building things that make an impact. Whether it's crafting high-scale software, tinkering with electronics, or contributing to open-source projects, I'm always exploring new ways to create, automate, and optimize.

## What I Do

I'm passionate about designing and developing tools that improve workflows, enhance security, and bring ideas to life. My expertise spans backend development, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and security, but I also dive into AI/ML, automation, and home tech just for fun. I believe in writing clean, efficient, and scalable code while always keeping security in mind.

## Hobbies & Interests

Beyond coding, you'll often find me:

-   🔧 **Tinkering with hardware** – I love working with **Raspberry Pi, Arduino, and Home Assistant**, building smart home automations and DIY electronics projects.
    
-   🛠 **Developing tools & open-source projects** – Whether it's automating tasks, improving DevOps workflows, or creating useful CLI tools, I enjoy building and sharing my work.
    
-   🤖 **Exploring AI/ML** – I find AI fascinating and love experimenting with models, automation, and machine learning applications.
    
-   🛡 **Application Security & White Hat Hacking** – I'm highly knowledgeable in **offensive and defensive security**, with deep expertise in **secure coding, vulnerability research, penetration testing, and security automation**. I actively work with **SAST/DAST tools, threat modeling, and exploit mitigation** to enhance security across applications and infrastructure.
    
-   🎮 **Gaming** – From classic Pokémon games to strategy and adventure titles, gaming has always been a passion of mine.
    
-   ✍️ **Blogging & sharing knowledge** – I enjoy writing about tech, security, and development best practices, aiming to help others in the community.
